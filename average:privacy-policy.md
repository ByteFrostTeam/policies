# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 2, 2026**

This policy describes how Average ("the app," "we," "us") handles information when you use it. If you only read one section, read "In short" below — the rest of this document just spells it out in full.

## In short

- Average has no backend. There is no server for your answers to go to.
- Everything you type into the app — your age, income, weight, sleep habits, and every other answer — is stored only on your device, in the app's local storage.
- We do not receive, see, collect, or have access to any of it, ever.
- The only outside services this app talks to are Apple's App Store / Google's Play Store and RevenueCat, and only to process your one-time purchase and let you restore it later. They do not receive your profile answers.
- There is no account, no email, no login, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.
- Sharing a result image is something you choose to do, each time, through your device's own share sheet. The app never posts or sends anything on your behalf.
- Deleting the app deletes your data. (See "How long we keep your information" for one honest caveat about device backups.)

## Information the app collects

Average asks you a series of questions during setup so it can compare your answers to population statistics. Depending on what you choose to answer, this can include:

- **Profile:** age, sex, country
- **Financial information:** annual income, savings, debt, housing costs, home value, mortgage balance
- **Health and body information:** height, weight, resting heart rate
- **Activity information:** workouts per week, workout length, daily step count, self-rated fitness
- **Daily habits:** sleep hours, alcohol consumption, cigarettes per day, coffee intake, screen time, commute time, hours worked per week
- **Household information:** number of people in your household, home size

Every question is optional to skip except a small required set needed to run the comparisons at all (age, sex, country). You can change or clear any answer at any time from within the app.

**About the health and financial information above:** we're calling this out specifically, separate from the general list, because we take it seriously. Nothing in this category is ever transmitted anywhere by this app. It is used only to compute the percentiles and score shown back to you, on your own device, and nowhere else.

## How this information is used

All of it stays on your device and is used only to:

- Calculate where your answers fall relative to population statistics bundled inside the app itself (not fetched from any server)
- Calculate your overall score and category scores
- Show you a history of how your answers and scores have changed over time, if you choose to update them later
- Generate a share image or share text, only when you explicitly tap "Share" — this image is created on your device and is only sent anywhere once you choose a destination in your device's own share sheet

We do not use your information for advertising, do not sell it, do not share it with data brokers, and do not use it to train any model, because we never receive it in the first place.

## Where your information is stored

Locally, on your device, using standard iOS/Android app storage. There is no cloud sync, no backup service operated by us, and no account system tying your data to an identity we hold.

## Who else sees anything

Two categories of outside service are involved in running this app, and neither receives your profile answers (age, income, health information, or anything else you type in):

- **Apple App Store / Google Play Store and RevenueCat**, to process the app's one-time purchase and let you restore it if you reinstall or switch devices. This involves purchase and device/account identifiers necessary for that transaction — standard for any paid app — not the information you enter about yourself. See [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), and [RevenueCat's privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy) for how each of them separately handles that transaction data.
- **HealthKit or a similar device health service**, only if and when the app adds an autofill option for a field like steps or sleep, and only after you separately grant that permission. As of this version, that autofill option does not exist in the app; this section will be updated if that changes, and any such data would be used the same way as everything else in this policy: locally, never transmitted.

We do not use any analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting service. There is no tracking SDK of any kind in this app.

## Sharing a result

The app can generate a share image (your score, or a single stat) so you can post it yourself. This only happens when you tap the share button. The image and any prefilled caption text are created on your device; you choose whether to send it, edit it, or cancel, using your device's own native share sheet. The app never posts, sends, or shares anything without you actively choosing a destination for it in that moment.

## How long we keep your information

For as long as it's useful to you: your answers persist in the app's local storage until you either change them, use the app's "restart onboarding" option, or delete the app. Deleting the app removes its local storage and, with it, everything you entered.

One honest caveat: if your device is set up to back up app data (for example, an iCloud or similar device backup), your answers may be included in that backup like any other app's local storage would be. That means restoring an old backup onto a new device could bring your answers back even after you deleted the app and started over. This is a property of your device's own backup system, not something Average does independently — the app itself never transmits, syncs, or stores your information anywhere outside the backup mechanism your device already provides.

## Children's privacy

Average is not directed at, or intended for use by, children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the app and you'd like their locally-stored data removed, deleting the app from that device removes it — see "How long we keep your information" above.

## Your rights and choices

Because your information is processed only on your own device and is never transmitted to us, many of the rights that data protection laws (such as the EU/UK GDPR, and US state laws including the CCPA/CPRA) are designed to guarantee — access, correction, deletion, and portability of your data — are already available to you directly, at any time, inside the app itself: every answer is visible and editable in the edit screen, and deleting the app deletes all of it.

If you have a question or request about privacy that isn't covered by using those in-app controls, contact us at contact@byte-frost.com.

## Changes to this policy

If what the app collects, or who it talks to, ever changes, we'll update this policy and change the "Last updated" date at the top. We won't quietly start doing something this policy says we don't do.
