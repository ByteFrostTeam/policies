Account Deletion - Marbly: Habit Tracker

This page explains how to delete your Marbly account and what data will be removed.

⚠️ Important: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data will be permanently deleted from our servers.

How to Delete Your Account

For users signed in with email:

  1. Open the Marbly app
  2. Go to Settings (tap the gear icon)
  3. Navigate to the Privacy & Security section
  4. Tap Delete account
  5. Confirm your decision in the confirmation dialog

For anonymous users (using the app without signing in):

Your data is stored on our servers with a random identifier but is not linked to any personal information (no email, name, or identifiable data). To delete your anonymous account data, please contact us at contact@byte-frost.com and we will delete your data within 30 days.

Alternatively, uninstalling the app will prevent you from accessing your data, though it will remain on our servers until you request deletion.

What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently removed:

  • Your authentication credentials (email and password)
  • All your goals and habits
  • All progress tracking data
  • Your marble collection and achievements
  • Any synchronized health data
  • Your subscription information (through RevenueCat)

Deletion is completed within 30 days as stated in our Privacy Policy.

Active Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel active subscriptions. To cancel your subscription:

  • Android: Go to Google Play Store → Subscriptions → Marbly → Cancel
  • iOS: Go to Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → Marbly → Cancel

Need Help?

If you're unable to delete your account through the app or have questions about data deletion, please contact us:

Email: contact@byte-frost.com

We will respond within 30 days and assist you with account deletion.
