Account Deletion - Marbly

This page explains how to delete your account and associated data from Marbly.

⚠️ Warning: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data will be permanently removed from our servers.

How to Delete Your Account

For users with email accounts:

  1. Open the Marbly app
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Navigate to Privacy & Security
  4. Tap "Delete account"
  5. Confirm your decision

For anonymous users:

  1. Open the Marbly app
  2. Go to Settings
  3. In the Account section, tap the copy icon next to "User ID"
  4. Email the User ID to contact@byte-frost.com
  5. We will delete your data within 30 days

What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, all of the following is permanently removed:

  • Account credentials (email and password, or anonymous user ID)
  • Goals and habits
  • Progress tracking data
  • Marble collections and achievements
  • Synchronized health data
  • Subscription records

Subscription Cancellation

Deleting your account does not cancel active subscriptions. To cancel:

  • Android: Google Play Store → Subscriptions → Marbly → Cancel
  • iOS: Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → Marbly → Cancel

Need Assistance?

If you need help with account deletion, contact us at contact@byte-frost.com

We will assist you with your request promptly.
