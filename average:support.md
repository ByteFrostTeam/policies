# Support

Need help with Average?

Email us at contact@byte-frost.com and we'll get back to you.

## Common questions

**Is my data private?**
Yes. Average has no account, no login, and no server. Everything you enter stays on your device. Deleting the app deletes your data.

**How do I restore my purchase on a new device?**
Open the app. If you haven't unlocked the report yet, tap "Restore purchases" on the paywall screen.

**Can I switch between metric and imperial units?**
Yes, in Settings.

**I found a bug, or have feedback**
Email contact@byte-frost.com — we read every message.
